Schools

Regional School District #4

Regional School District #4 serves Chester, Deep River, and Essex.

Central office

  • Address 1 Winthrop Road, Deep River, CT 06417
  • Phone 860-526-2417
  • Website reg4.k12.ct.us
  • District reg4.k12.ct.us/about-the-district

Hours & calendars

  • School hours reg4.k12.ct.us/school-hours
  • Calendars reg4.k12.ct.us/calendars
  • Elementary ees.reg4.k12.ct.us

The district publishes 1,306 students enrolled PreK–12 and a 98% graduation rate on its site. Valley Regional High School and John Winthrop Middle School are in neighboring Deep River and serve Essex students; Essex Elementary School, at ees.reg4.k12.ct.us, provides the town’s elementary education.

Utilities

Verify your providers

The Town of Essex does not publish a utilities page of its own, so verify your providers directly:

Electricity
Eversource is Connecticut’s dominant electric utility — eversource.com. Confirm the provider for your specific address.
Water
The CT Public Utilities Regulatory Authority publishes water utilities by town (portal.ct.gov — PURA “Water Utilities by Town”). Aquarion Water Company (aquarionwater.com) is a major Connecticut water utility; confirm whether it serves your address.
Gas and sewer
Not confirmed. Ask the town’s Public Works department or check the PURA list.

Transit

River Valley Transit and Shoreline East

River Valley Transit, operated by the Estuary Transit District, provides public transit for the Lower Connecticut River Valley, including Essex.

Website
rivervalleytransit.com
Schedules
rivervalleytransit.com/schedules/
XtraMile microtransit
rivervalleytransit.com/xtramile/
Services
Local fixed-route bus, Dial-A-Ride, XtraMile microtransit, and ADA paratransit, across 12 routes district-wide.

Shoreline East provides commuter rail along the shoreline, with the nearest stations to Essex in the Old Saybrook and Westbrook area; Old Saybrook station is the Shoreline East and Amtrak hub for this part of the river valley.
