Recurring events

Burning of the Ships Commemoration

The Sailing Masters of 1812 commemorate the British raid on Essex of 1814 on the second Saturday in May every year.

CT Spring Boat Show

A three-day spring boat show in Essex, listed for April 25–27 and a fundraiser for Sails Up 4 Cancer.

Memorial Day Parade

Features the Essex Elementary School band.

North Pole Express

The Essex Steam Train’s holiday experience each winter.

Ivoryton Playhouse season

An annual spring-to-fall mainstage season. The 2026 season includes A Piece of My Heart, October 1–25, 2026.

Essex Library Association programs

Year-round author talks, children’s programs, a film festival, and summer reading.

Examples of coverage seen on October 6, 2026

These illustrate the kind of news the feeds carry — they are not current headlines.

  • CT Insider reported that the LifeStar helicopter ambulance service was called to a crash in Essex on a Monday morning.
  • Patch reported that former Connecticut Governor Dannel P. Malloy — now Chancellor of the University of Maine System — was to speak at the Essex Library, and that the three-day CT Spring Boat Show aims to raise $30,000 for Sails Up 4 Cancer.
  • NewsBreak reported that Brien Riedell leads the Essex Historical Society’s Essex Village Walking Tours, covering the history, architecture, and evolution of one of Connecticut’s best-preserved historic villages.
