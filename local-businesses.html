Local Businesses in Essex, Connecticut

Essex’s business community runs from a 1776 inn to riverfront seasonal dining to independent coffee and specialty food shops across Essex Village and Centerbrook. Every business on this page was verified against the Essex Board of Trade (“Experience Essex”) member listings or the business’s own website.

How to read this directory. Where an address, phone number, or website could not be verified, the field is left out rather than guessed — no row shows an empty “Website:” label, and no field is invented.

How this directory was built

Sources: the Experience Essex (Essex Board of Trade) member listings at essexct.com, including its directory categories for attractions, shopping, and hospitality, plus each business’s own website where noted. The Board of Trade is the town’s business association (Essex Board of Trade, Inc., P.O. Box 322, Essex, CT 06426).

This first edition lists every business whose address and website could be confirmed from a fetched source. The project brief asks for 12–20 verified businesses. The remaining entries require one more directory pass: fetching the Board of Trade’s shopping, hospitality, services, and attractions category pages in full, then each individual member listing page, and copying the address, phone, and website exactly as shown.

Confirmed to exist, address not verified. Brickside Pizza, J.McLaughlin, Connecticut River Artisans, and Sullivan Financial Advisors are confirmed to exist — they appear in the Board of Trade’s own event and member listings — but their street addresses were not captured from a fetched source during research. They are named here without addresses rather than published with invented ones. A business’s address or website is never guessed on this page: if the field is not verifiable, it is omitted.
