Visit Essex

Things to Do in Essex, Connecticut

Essex packs a working historic riverfront, three landmark attractions, and a web of town trails into one small town on the Connecticut River. Here is what to see, where to walk, and what happens each year.

Landmarks & attractions

Four landmarks on the river and in the villages

Heritage rail

Essex Steam Train & Riverboat

A heritage railroad and riverboat operating out of Essex Village, run by The Valley Railroad Company. Signature experiences include riverboat excursions and the holiday North Pole Express.

  • Website essexsteamtrain.com

Confirm the exact station street number and current schedule at essexsteamtrain.com before you travel.

Museum

Connecticut River Museum

A history and nature museum on the Connecticut River, housed in a historic 1878 steamboat warehouse on the Essex waterfront.

  • Address 67 Main Street, Essex, CT 06426
  • Phone 860-767-8269
  • Email crm@ctrivermuseum.org
  • Website ctrivermuseum.org
Historic inn

The Griswold Inn (established 1776)

One of America’s oldest continuously operating inns, with a tap room and restaurant on Main Street.

  • Address 36 Main Street, Essex, CT 06426
  • Website griswoldinn.com
Theatre

Ivoryton Playhouse

A historic professional theatre in Ivoryton with an annual season. The 2026 season includes Shirley Lauro’s A Piece of My Heart, running October 1–25, 2026.

  • Website ivorytonplayhouse.org
Village library

Ivoryton Library

A separate library serving the Ivoryton village, with its own programs.

Confirm the current address and hours before visiting.

Parks, preserves & trails

Walk the town’s open space

The town’s parks and nature preserves page is the starting point for maps and trail information: essexct.gov/247/Parks-Nature-Preserves-in-Essex.

Park

Essex Park

A public park on the river, with town-published amenities and park maps.

Preserve

Bushy Hill Nature Preserve

Mapped preserve with plant and animal descriptions (town-hosted PDF).

Preserve

Canfield-Meadow Woods Nature Preserve

Hiking trails, entry points, and wildlife information (town-hosted PDF).

Preserve

Viney Hill Brook Preserve

Hiking trails, picnicking, and a paw park; beaver activity at Quarry and Wollock’s Ponds (town-hosted PDF).

Trail

Town-owned bike path

Mapped route that includes public kayak launches and hiking trails (town-hosted PDF).

Land trust

Essex Land Trust preserves

The Land Trust maintains additional preserves with trails, including a 104-acre preserve with six trails. Details at essexlandtrust.org/preserve-profiles/.

Recreation programs. The Park & Recreation Commission page is essexct.gov/225/Park-Recreation-Commission, and program registration runs through essexct.recdesk.com.

Annual & seasonal events

What happens every year in Essex

Burning of the Ships Commemoration

The Sailing Masters of 1812 commemorate the British raid on Essex of 1814 on the second Saturday in May every year.

North Pole Express

The Essex Steam Train’s holiday experience each winter.

Ivoryton Playhouse season

A spring-to-fall mainstage season each year.

Essex Library programs

Year-round author talks, children’s programs, a film festival, and summer reading.

CT Spring Boat Show

A three-day spring boat show in Essex (April 25–27 on the Patch listing captured during research), a fundraiser for Sails Up 4 Cancer.

Memorial Day Parade

Features the Essex Elementary School band.
